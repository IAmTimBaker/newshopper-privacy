NewsHopper Support

NewsHopper is a Safari extension for Mac that opens web articles in the News app.

Contact

Questions, bugs or feature requests: email NewsHopper Support. Please include your macOS version and the link to the article if something didn't open.

Getting started

  1. Open the NewsHopper app once, then click Quit and Open Safari Settings…
  2. In Safari Settings → Extensions, check NewsHopper.
  3. On any article, click the NewsHopper button in Safari's toolbar. You can also right-click a link and choose Open Link in News.
  4. Optional: to open a site's articles in News automatically, go to Safari Settings → Extensions → NewsHopper → Settings and add the site, for example theverge.com.

Common questions

I don't see the NewsHopper button in Safari.

Make sure NewsHopper is checked in Safari Settings → Extensions. If it's on but you still can't see the button, choose View → Customize Toolbar and drag the NewsHopper button into the toolbar.

An article opened in News, but News shows an error or a different page.

News can open an article only if its publisher is in Apple News. Some articles also need an Apple News+ subscription. If the publisher isn't in News, read the article in Safari instead.

How do I keep an article in Safari when automatic opening is on?

Load the same link again within one minute, by going back and clicking it again or by reloading, and NewsHopper leaves it in Safari.

Articles from a site I added don't open automatically.

When you add a site, Safari asks you to let NewsHopper access it. If you said no, remove the site and add it again, then allow access. You can also check this in Safari Settings → Extensions → NewsHopper → Edit Websites…

NewsHopper sends only articles to News. Homepages and section pages, like theverge.com or theverge.com/tech, stay in Safari.

How do I turn off automatic opening?

In NewsHopper's settings, uncheck Automatically open articles from these sites, or remove a site by clicking Remove next to it. The toolbar button and the right-click menu keep working.

How do I uninstall NewsHopper?

Quit Safari, then drag the NewsHopper app from your Applications folder to the Trash. This removes the extension and its settings.

Does NewsHopper collect my data?

No. NewsHopper has no accounts, analytics or tracking, and your settings stay on your Mac. See the Privacy Policy.

Requirements

A Mac with Safari and the News app. To open some articles you need an Apple News+ subscription.
